Population aged 16+, from the annual census

Loading census data…

From INE's annual census, which takes work status from Social Security and employment-office registers, not from a survey. "Other inactive" is everyone who is neither working, registered as unemployed, receiving a pension nor studying: people looking after the home or family, people out of work but not registered, and so on.

Why these numbers differ from the EPA charts above
  • Registers, not a survey. "In work" here means registered with Social Security or in tax records, so informal work is not counted. "Unemployed" means registered as a job seeker at an employment office. The EPA asks people what they did, so it counts both.
  • Unregistered job seekers count as inactive. That is why the activity and unemployment rates here are lower than the EPA's (on 1 January 2024: 55% of people born abroad were active and 13% of those were unemployed; in the EPA for 2024 Q1, 69% and 19% of foreign nationals).
  • Country of birth, not nationality. Born abroad includes people who have become Spanish, many of them older or retired. Foreign nationals include children of immigrants born in Spain. The two groups are about the same size but are not the same people.
  • One day, not a quarter. The census is a snapshot on 1 January; the EPA is a quarterly estimate.
  • Which to use: the EPA for unemployment rates and change over time; the census to compare countries of birth and provinces, where the EPA sample is too small.

Benefits

SEPE's unemployment benefits are either contributory, paid for by the worker's previous contributions, or means-tested unemployment subsidies. Other social assistance is separate and not counted in them: the Minimum Living Income (IMV), a national income for low-income households that requires at least one year of legal residence; regional minimum incomes such as the Basque RGI; and family, housing and local aid. This page shows the IMV here and the RGI under Basque Country.

Foreign recipients of unemployment benefits

Annual average, by type. Label: share of all recipients who are foreign

Contributory: for people who lost a job after paying contributions for at least a year; paid for up to two years, based on past wages. Unemployment subsidies (SEPE's assistance level): a flat, means-tested payment for people whose contributory benefit has run out or who did not contribute long enough; includes the farm-worker subsidies in Andalusia and Extremadura. RAI: a means-tested income with job-search help for groups who find work hard: long-term unemployed over 45, people with disabilities, victims of gender violence, returning emigrants. Employment Activation Programme: a temporary payment with job-search support for long-term unemployed people with family responsibilities who had no other benefit, 2015–2020.
